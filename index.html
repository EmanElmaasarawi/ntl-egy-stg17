    Where is measurable activity concentrated?

    National Sum of Lights grew +0.6% per year between 2015 and 2024. The light-to-population elasticity across ADM2 units is 0.38 (95% CI 0.32–0.45, R²=0.27): light is spread more evenly than population, suggesting broad-based service coverage.

    Who lives without detectable light?

    1,120,336 people — 1.2% of the inhabited population — live in cells that are inhabited but emit no detectable night-time light, against 1,064,192 in 2015. The largest concentrations are listed in the table; they are the natural starting point for a targeted off-grid or grid-extension programme. Depending on the noise floor (0.15–0.5 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹), this figure ranges from 1,108,542 to 1,211,041: that is the indicator's real uncertainty.

    How did the lit footprint change?

    The lit footprint moved from 90,477 to 116,532 km² (+28.8%), with 31,084 km² newly lit and 5,028 km² losing detectable light. The sprawl ratio of 3.71 indicates a pattern of outward spread rather than densification.

    Is light concentrating or spreading?

    Population-weighted inequality in light per capita across ADM2 units widened from 0.429 to 0.535 (Gini). 26% of total inequality sits between ADM1 regions and 74% within them. Initially brighter units grew faster, indicating divergence.

    Which districts changed significantly?

    Median ADM2 growth in Sum of Lights is -1.2% per year. Growth is measured between the median of the first and last three years. 19 unit(s) exceed the robust +2.5σ threshold and 1 fall below −2.5σ; these are candidates for investigation, not confirmed findings — each should be checked against the gas-flare list, the product version and any known local event. 37 unit(s) with a very low initial base are shown but never flagged. Neighbouring districts grow alike (Moran's I = 0.46): flagged changes often cluster and should be read as regional rather than local phenomena.

    Limitations — read before citing

    • Thresholds: noise floor 0.25, urban core 10.0 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹; every 'unlit' figure depends on the first of these.
    • The brightest 1 % of pixels hold 59.1% of the national Sum of Lights.
    • Gas-flare candidates detected: 20. Verify against petroleum infrastructure before interpreting.
    • Night-time lights do not measure electricity access, GDP or population — every such reading is an inference.
    • Activity that emits no light is invisible here: subsistence agriculture, daytime informal trade, indoor activity.
    • No DMSP-OLS year is compared with a VIIRS year; the two are different physical quantities.
